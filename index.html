I'm an AI safety researcher working on the failure modes of large language models. I'm a Research Assistant at Mila, where I work with Prof. Golnoosh Farnadi, and a Member of Technical Staff at Skyfall AI, where I work on dynamic world modeling and on optimizing LLM utilization and distillation.

My research interests are memorization and unlearning, the off-target effects of fine-tuning, adversarial robustness and red-teaming, prompt injection, model evaluation, and mechanistic interpretability — broadly, understanding what a model does that nobody intended, and building the evaluations that surface it. My work with Prof. Farnadi on the off-target effects of fine-tuning is supported by a grant from Coefficient Giving.

Previously I was a Technical AI Safety & Governance Research Fellow at the Oxford Martin AI Governance Initiative, and an ML Research Engineer at DynamoFL, where I built LLM red-teaming and adversarial evaluation systems. I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science (AI stream) from the University of Windsor, where I was a Vector Scholar in AI. My work has appeared at EMNLP, NAACL, and EACL.

Outside of work I read across diverse topics — novels, political science, and history. I competed in track and field through high school and my first two years of university, and I still run. More recently I've been getting into hiking.

Always happy to chat or collaborate — email me.
